MLFMA.Harmonics — Module
MLFMA.Harmonics

Orthonormal spherical and circular harmonics, their transforms and storage helpers.

The submodules are available as MLFMA.SphericalHarmonics and MLFMA.CircularHarmonics, but are not exported from MLFMA.
